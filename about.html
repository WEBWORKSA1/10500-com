About 10500 关于我们

一万零五百: ten-thousand (万, “myriad”) plus the Five Blessings (五福). We picked a number that stands for abundance, and set out to make the world's most useful site about Chinese numbers.

Our mission

Numbers decide how products are priced, which floors get built, what goes into a wedding envelope, and how a deal is signed across the Chinese-speaking world. Most of that knowledge is scattered, simplified or wrong. We want to make it practical, accurate and free.

What we believe

  • Tools first. Answer the question in seconds, then explain it.
  • Sources, not superstition. We explain why a number carries meaning (usually a homophone) and cite official data such as the National Bureau of Statistics.
  • Respect. Folk beliefs are cultural heritage. We explain them without mocking them and without overselling them.
  • Independence. We're funded by ads, sponsors and readers, and sponsored content is always labelled.

Editorial standards

Articles are researched against primary sources and reviewed for linguistic accuracy. Corrections are welcome. Tell us and we'll fix errors quickly and note material changes.
