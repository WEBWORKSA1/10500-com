Red Envelope Calculator 红包

Never over-give or under-give again. Pick the occasion, region and relationship, and get a culturally appropriate, lucky amount that avoids unlucky 4s.

Red envelope etiquette in 60 seconds

  • Colour: red for happy occasions and white for funerals. Never mix them up.
  • Amount: even numbers for celebrations (weddings especially), and anything with 6, 8 or 9. No 4s.
  • Notes: new and crisp. Avoid coins and avoid mixing many small notes.
  • Giving: use both hands, with a greeting: 恭喜发财 (New Year), 百年好合 (weddings).
  • Receiving: don't open it in front of the giver.
  • Digital: WeChat and Alipay red packets are normal in mainland China. The same number rules apply, and 520 and 1314 are popular romantic amounts.
