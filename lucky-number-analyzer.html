How Chinese number luck works

Chinese number symbolism rests on homophones, words that sound alike. 八 (bā, 8) sounds like 发 (fā, to prosper), so 8 is prized. 四 (sì, 4) sounds like 死 (sǐ, death), so it is avoided. 六 (liù, 6) sounds like 溜 (smooth), and 九 (jiǔ, 9) sounds like 久 (long-lasting).

Combinations read like short phrases. 168 (yī liù bā) is heard as 一路发, “prosper all the way”. 518 is 我要发, “I will prosper”. 514 is 我要死, “I'm going to die”, and nobody wants that on their licence plate.

Tips for choosing a lucky number

  1. Protect the ending: the last 2–4 digits carry the most weight.
  2. Replace 4s where you can, and avoid 14, 54, 74 and 514 in particular.
  3. Look for phrases: 168, 518, 1688, 5188, 6868 and 998.
  4. Match the purpose: 520 and 1314 suit romance, 8s suit business, and 9s suit longevity and anniversaries.
  5. Consider resale value. Buyers in Chinese-speaking markets pay extra for 8s and avoid 4s.
