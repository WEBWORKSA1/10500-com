How Chinese payroll deductions work

  1. Social insurance (五险): the employee pays about 10.5% (pension 8%, medical 2%, unemployment 0.5%). The employer pays considerably more.
  2. Housing provident fund (公积金): 5–12%, matched by the employer.
  3. Individual income tax (个税): charged on salary minus the above, minus ¥5,000, minus special additional deductions.
