# 10500 — Research Brief: Chinese Culture, Economy & People

## 1. How 10500 is read in Chinese

| Form | Text | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Standard numeral | 一万零五百 (yī wàn líng wǔ bǎi) | 万 = 10,000 is a *base unit* in Chinese (Western 1,000 has no equivalent unit), so 10,500 = "one wan, zero, five hundred". |
| Financial (大写) | 壹万零伍佰 (元整) | Anti-fraud "capital" numerals used on cheques, invoices, contracts, bank slips. |
| Traditional | 一萬零五百 / 壹萬零伍佰 | Hong Kong, Taiwan, Macau, diaspora. |
| Digit-by-digit | 幺零五零零 (yāo líng wǔ líng líng) | Phone / room / code reading uses *yāo* for 1. |
| **The learner trap** | 一万五 = **15,000**, not 10,500 | Colloquial 一万五 drops the trailing unit (千). Getting 10,500 right requires the 零 (zero). This makes "10500" a genuine teaching hook for every Mandarin learner. |

## 2. Cultural symbolism

- **万 (wàn, 10,000)** = "myriad / everything / countless". Core of blessings: 万事如意 (may all go as wished), 万岁 (long live), 万福 (ten-thousand blessings).
- **五 (wǔ, 5)** links to 五福 *wǔfú*, the Five Blessings (longevity, wealth, health/peace, virtue, a peaceful end), and to 五行 *wǔxíng*, the Five Elements. Some sources flag 5 as mildly negative (homophone of 无 "none"); context decides.
- **零 (líng, 0)** stands for completeness and wholeness; in phone numbers it is neutral.
- **500 (五百)**: 五百罗汉, the 500 Arhats of Chinese Buddhism.
- **Numerology economy is real money:** 8888-8888 phone number sold for ¥2.33M in Chengdu (Aug 2003, Guinness); Hong Kong plate "28" sold for HK$18.1M (~US$2.3M, 2016, Bloomberg); plates 18 and 9 fetched ~US$2.1M and ~US$1.7M. Buildings skip 4th floors; wedding gifts avoid 4; prices end in 8.
- **Number slang is mainstream:** 520 = "I love you" (May 20 unofficial Valentine's), 1314 = "forever", 88 = bye-bye, 250 = fool, 666 = "smooth/awesome".

## 3. Economic significance

- **¥10,500 ≈ the average monthly wage of China's urban non-private sector.** NBS: 2024 average annual wage ¥124,110 (≈ ¥10,343/mo); 2025 ¥129,441 (≈ ¥10,787/mo). Private units 2025: ¥71,590 (≈ ¥5,966/mo). So "a ¥10,500 month" is a meaningful benchmark for salary, cost-of-living, relocation and expat searches.
- **10.5% = the typical employee social-insurance deduction** in China (pension 8% + medical 2% + unemployment 0.5%), a second natural "10500" hook for a salary calculator.
- **Numeric domains are a Chinese web tradition:** 163.com (NetEase), 58.com, 360.cn, 4399.com, 51job.com, 12306.cn, hao123.com. Numbers are easier than Latin letters for Chinese users, so a 5-digit .com is brandable in China.
