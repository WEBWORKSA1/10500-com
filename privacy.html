Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

1. Who we are

10500.com (“10500”, “we”) is an independent online publication. You can reach us through our contact page.

2. What we collect

  • Information you give us: name, email, company, phone or messaging ID, and message content when you submit a form (concierge, contact, newsletter, contest, careers, donation pledge, advertising inquiry).
  • Tool inputs: numbers and dates you type into our tools are processed in your browser and are not sent to our servers. They may appear in the page URL so you can share results.
  • Technical data: with consent, analytics (Google Analytics 4) collects anonymized usage data. Ad partners may collect device identifiers.
  • Attribution: the landing page, referrer and campaign (UTM) parameters, attached to form submissions so we can understand what's working.

3. How we use it

To reply to you, deliver requested services and newsletters, match concierge requests with specialists (only with your request), run contests, improve the site, show ads and prevent abuse.

4. Service providers

Forms are delivered through a form-processing provider (FormSubmit) and email. Hosting is provided by GitHub Pages. Payments are handled by the provider you choose (e.g., PayPal, Ko-fi, Stripe), and we never receive card numbers. Embedded videos load from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode only when you press play.

5. Cookies & advertising

We use browser storage for preferences (theme, cookie choice). With your consent we load Google Analytics and personalised Google AdSense ads. Without consent, ads that do load are non-personalised.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

You can change your choice at any time by clearing this site's storage in your browser.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share concierge request details with a matched specialist only to fulfil your request. We may disclose information if required by law.

7. Retention

Form messages are kept for up to 24 months, or longer where needed for an active business relationship. Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (EU/UK GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA, Canada PIPEDA/Québec Law 25, and others), you can access, correct, delete or port your data, object to processing, and withdraw consent. Use the contact form and select “Privacy request”. We don't discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

9. Children

The site is not directed to children under 13 (16 in the EU), and contests are for adults only.

10. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. No method is 100% secure, but we limit the data we collect to what's needed.

11. Changes

We'll update this page and its date when practices change.
