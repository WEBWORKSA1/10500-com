How Chinese numbers are built

Chinese counts in groups of four digits, not three. The key units are 十 (10), 百 (100), 千 (1,000), 万 (10,000) and 亿 (100,000,000). So 10,500 is “one 万, zero, five 百” (一万零五百), and 1,000,000 is 一百万 (a hundred 万).

Writing money in 大写: step by step

  1. Write the integer part in capital numerals: 10500 → 壹万零伍佰.
  2. Add 元 (yuan).
  3. Add 角 (0.1) and 分 (0.01) if present: .05 → 零伍分.
  4. If there are no cents, finish with 整: 壹万零伍佰元整.
  5. On a cheque, write 人民币 before the amount and leave no gaps.
